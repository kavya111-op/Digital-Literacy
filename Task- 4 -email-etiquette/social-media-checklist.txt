Do’s for Responsible Social Media Use

Be respectful and polite in all posts and comments

Think before posting—consider long-term impact

Protect your privacy (limit personal information shared)

Verify information before sharing (avoid spreading misinformation)

Maintain a professional image (future employers may view your profile)

❌ Don’ts for Responsible Social Media Use

Don’t post offensive, abusive, or hateful content

Don’t share fake news or unverified information

Don’t overshare personal details (address, phone number, etc.)

Don’t engage in online arguments or trolling

Don’t post inappropriate photos or content that can harm your reputation

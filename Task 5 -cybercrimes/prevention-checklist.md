 Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist

* General Safety Tips

Use strong and unique passwords for all accounts
 Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever possible
 Do not click on suspicious links or unknown emails/messages
 Always verify the sender before sharing any personal information
 Keep your apps and devices updated regularly
 Avoid using public Wi-Fi for logging into important accounts
 Check website URLs carefully before entering login details
 Do not share OTPs, passwords, or personal details with anyone

*UPI / Financial Safety Tips

Never approve unknown UPI collect requests
Always double-check the receiver’s name before making payments
Do not scan random QR codes sent by strangers
Remember: receiving money does NOT require entering a UPI PIN

*Report Cybercrime

National Cyber Crime Portal: https://cybercrime.gov.in
Helpline Number: 1930

While researching cybercrime, I was surprised by how easily people become victims of online fraud, especially UPI scams. Many fraud messages and calls look extremely genuine, making it difficult to identify them as fake. I realized that even a small mistake, such as clicking on a suspicious link or approving a payment request, can lead to serious financial loss.
This research made me more aware of the importance of being cautious while using digital platforms. One habit I will personally change is that I will always verify any payment request or message before taking action. I will also avoid clicking on unknown links and ensure that I never share sensitive information like OTPs or UPI PINs with anyone.

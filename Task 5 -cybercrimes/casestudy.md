UPI / Online payment fraud
UPI (Unified Payments Interface) fraud is a rapidly increasing cybercrime in India, where attackers trick users into sending money or revealing sensitive information. This type of fraud mainly targets students, elderly people, and individuals who are not fully aware of digital payment security.
A common scenario begins when a victim receives a phone call or message from someone pretending to be a bank official, delivery agent, or customer support executive. The fraudster creates urgency by claiming an issue with the victim’s account or offering a reward. The victim is then asked to scan a QR code, click on a link, or approve a “collect request” on their UPI app.
Step-by-step, the fraud occurs as follows: first, the attacker gains trust by impersonation; second, they send a payment request or malicious link; third, the victim unknowingly approves the request or enters sensitive details like OTP or UPI PIN; finally, money is deducted from the victim’s account.
The consequences of such fraud include immediate financial loss, stress, and possible misuse of personal data. In many cases, victims find it difficult to recover their money.This case highlights the importance of awareness, verifying requests, and never sharing confidential information while using digital payment systems.
